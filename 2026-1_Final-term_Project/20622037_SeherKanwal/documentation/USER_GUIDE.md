# 🦉 FinanceMentor AI — User Guide

Welcome to **FinanceMentor AI** — your personal AI-powered financial education platform. This guide will walk you through every feature of the app in plain language so you can get the most out of it from day one.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

When you first open the app, you'll land on the **Dashboard**. You'll notice:

- A **sidebar on the left** — this is where your AI Mentor 🦉 always lives.
- A **navigation menu** in the sidebar — use this to move between sections.
- A **dark-themed interface** with live market data loading automatically.

> [!TIP]
> The very first time you open the app, your portfolio is automatically pre-loaded with 8 popular stocks and crypto assets (AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, TSLA, AMZN, BTC, ETH, SOL) so you have real data to explore right away.

## 🦉 Your AI Mentor (Always in the Sidebar)

No matter which page you're on, the **FinanceMentor AI** panel is always visible in the left sidebar.

### What it does:
- Shows a **chat window** where you can ask any follow-up question about what you're looking at
- Remembers the **context of the current page** (e.g., if you're on the Market Terminal, it knows you're looking at price charts)
- Responds in a **warm, educational tone** — it never gives direct buy/sell advice

### How to use it:
1. Type any question in the chat box at the bottom of the sidebar
2. Hit **Enter** to send
3. The Owl 🦉 will reply with a concise, contextual answer
4. Use the **🗑️ Clear Chat** button to start a fresh conversation

### Developer Mode Toggle:
At the top of the sidebar there's a **"Developer Mode"** switch.
- **OFF (Beginner Mode)** → Charts show as simple line graphs with easy language
- **ON (Advanced Mode)** → Charts show candlesticks, technical indicators (MA20, volume), and more detailed data

> [!NOTE]
> You can switch modes any time — your data and progress are always saved.

## 📚 Learning Paths

**Where to find it:** Mentor & Learning → Learning Paths

Learn core financial concepts through structured lessons — and test yourself with AI-generated quizzes.

### Choose a Learning Path:

| Path | What you'll learn |
|------|-------------------|
| **Beginner Investor** | Stocks, compound interest, risk vs. reward |
| **Crypto Basics** | Blockchain, volatility, wallets & private keys |
| **Portfolio Building** | Diversification, time horizons, rebalancing |

### How lessons work:
1. Pick a path from the dropdown at the top
2. Click on any lesson to expand it and read the concept
3. Click **"🎓 Master this Concept"** when you feel you understand it — this marks it complete and updates your progress
4. ✅ marks appear next to lessons you've completed

### How quizzes work:
1. Click **"🤔 Quiz Me"** on any lesson
2. The AI generates a unique multiple-choice question for that concept
3. Select your answer and click **"Submit Answer"**
4. Whether right or wrong, the AI Mentor explains the answer and opens a follow-up chat so you can ask more
5. Click **"Close Quiz"** when done

> [!TIP]
> After mastering 5 or more concepts, a special button appears to launch the **Advanced Roleplay** scenario!

## 🎭 Finance Roleplay

**Where to find it:** Mentor & Learning → Finance Roleplay

This is the most immersive feature. The AI puts you inside a real financial scenario and challenges your decision-making.

### How to start:
1. Choose a scenario from the dropdown (or write your own custom one)
2. Click **"🚀 Start Roleplay"**
3. The Mentor AI sets the scene — read it carefully

### During the roleplay:
- Type your decision in the text box (e.g., *"I will hold my position and not panic sell"*)
- Click **"Submit Action"**
- The AI reacts to your choice, explains the likely consequences, and challenges your reasoning with follow-up questions (Socratic method)
- Keep going back and forth as long as you like

### Ending the session:
- Click **"🛑 End & Get Feedback"** at any time
- The AI reviews everything you said and gives you:
  - What you did well
  - Where you showed risk-blindness
  - An **Emotional Discipline score out of 10** 🎯

### Available scenarios:

| Scenario | The Situation |
|----------|---------------|
| The 20% Dip | Your portfolio just lost $1,000. Everyone is selling. |
| Crypto Moonshot | Your friend made a fortune on a coin you've never heard of. FOMO hits. |
| First Bull Market | The market is up 15%. You want to borrow money to invest more. |
| Inflation Spike | Inflation is 8%. Your savings are losing value daily. |
| Inheritance Windfall | You just received $50,000. Friends say YOLO it. |
| Job Loss Crisis | You're laid off with no emergency cash but a big portfolio. |
| Retirement Trap | You're 5 years from retirement and the market crashes. |
| ✨ Custom | Describe any financial situation you're facing. |

## 📈 Live Market Terminal

- Select any asset you hold from the dropdown

  - Live price + today's change

### How to use the chart controls:
1. Select an asset from the dropdown (or type a custom ticker)

### Chart modes:

- **Advanced Mode** → Candlestick chart with a 20-day Moving Average (MA20) and trading volume bars

Click **"🦉 Explain Chart"** on any chart to get an AI breakdown of what the price action means.

## 🏦 Paper Trading

**Where to find it:** Simulations → Paper Trading

Practice buying and selling with **virtual money** — no real money at risk.

### Starting balance:
You begin with **$10,000 in virtual cash** (resets each session).

### How to execute a trade:
1. Enter a **ticker symbol** (e.g., `AAPL` or `BTC-USD`)
2. Enter the **quantity** of shares/units
3. Select **BUY** or **SELL**
4. Click the execute button
5. The app fetches the **live real price** and completes the trade instantly

### Rules:
- You can't BUY if you don't have enough cash
- You can't SELL more than you hold
- All trades are confirmed with the exact price and cost

### Viewing your holdings:
Scroll down to see all your current positions — each shows the live price, today's % change, how many units you hold, and the current value.

> [!NOTE]
> Your paper trading balance and holdings **reset** when you close the browser or refresh — this is by design so you can always start fresh.

## 🧠 Decision Coach

**Where to find it:** Simulations → Decision Coach

Before making any real investment, use the Decision Coach to stress-test your thinking.

### The 5-step wizard:

**Step 1 — What's the Move?**
Describe the asset you're thinking of buying/selling and your reasoning (e.g., *"I want to buy $500 of NVIDIA because I think AI will grow a lot"*).

**Step 2 — Time Horizon**
How long do you plan to hold this investment?
- Short Term (< 1 Month)
- Intermediate (1–12 Months)
- Long Term (1–5 Years)
- Legacy (5+ Years)

**Step 3 — Worst Case**
Use the slider to set: *"If this drops X%, I would panic sell."* This reveals your true risk tolerance.

**Step 4 — Emotional State**
Be honest — how are you feeling right now?
- FOMO (excited/anxious to get in)
- Fear (worried about losing more)
- Logic (calm and calculated)

**Step 5 — AI Review**
The AI reads all your answers and gives you a structured critique:
- Summary of your reasoning
- What's logically sound in your plan
- Critical risks you haven't considered
- Overlooked factors (taxes, liquidity, correlation, etc.)
- What research you should do before deciding

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is **not financial advice** — it's a reasoning evaluator. Think of it as a devil's advocate that helps you think more clearly.

## 🔥 Crisis Simulator

**Where to find it:** Simulations → Crisis Simulator

Test your nerves with real-world market crisis scenarios.

### How it works:
1. A crisis scenario is presented in a bold orange alert card
2. Read the situation carefully
3. Choose from **4 options** — click your decision
4. The AI immediately evaluates your choice and explains:
   - Whether you picked the optimal response
   - The financial principle at play (e.g., loss aversion, panic selling, dollar-cost averaging)
5. Click **"Try Next Scenario"** to continue

### Navigating scenarios:
Use the **← Prev** and **Next →** buttons to move between the 4 scenarios in any order.

### Why this matters:
Most investors make their worst decisions during market crises — not because they lack knowledge, but because of **emotional reactions**. This simulator trains you to pause and think before acting.

**Where to find it:** Live Markets → Portfolio Tracker

A deeper view of your portfolio with risk analysis and AI reporting.

### Adding holdings:
Use the **"➕ Add / Edit Holding"** expander at the top — enter ticker, units, and average buy price.

### Performance Cards:
- **Current Value** — total portfolio value at live prices
- **Total P&L** — dollar profit/loss from cost basis
- **Daily Pulse** — today's overall movement

### Risk Intelligence Charts:
Two side-by-side charts:
- **Sector Diversification** — a donut chart showing your allocation across Technology, Consumer, Finance, and Crypto
- **Concentration Risk** — a bar chart showing which assets dominate your portfolio, color-coded by P&L

### Automatic Warnings:
The app highlights two risks automatically:
- ⚠️ **Concentration Warning** — if any single asset exceeds 40% of your portfolio
- ⚠️ **Sector Overexposure** — if Technology stocks exceed 60% of your total value

### AI Smart Report:
Click **"📡 Run Full AI Smart Report"** to get a professional-style portfolio risk assessment:
- Diversification gaps
- Sector exposure analysis
- Emotional discipline tips based on your risk profile
